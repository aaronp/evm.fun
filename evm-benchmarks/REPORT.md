### 2026-11-19: 01 vs 02, n=10000, 3 runs, webevm@0.9.1 default engine

Median TPS, 01 baseline / 02 seq-memory: atomic-swap 817 / 1,215; erc20-disjoint 1,300 / 2,566; erc20-hot 1,300 / 2,606; erc20-mint 1,432 / 3,135; eth-transfer 280 / 318. All runs verified, none rejected or reverted.

- 02 is 1.1x to 2.4x faster than 01 (the spike's ~10x for single-sender transfers did not carry over to these scenarios).
- The 1,000 TPS target is met by 02 on 4 of 5 scenarios and by 01 on 3 of 5 (erc20-disjoint, erc20-hot, erc20-mint). Neither meets it on eth-transfer.
- `mine()` dominates 02's time. eth-transfer sends to 10,000 fresh recipients and spends about 30 s in `mine()` per run (about 3 ms per recipient if spread evenly; derived, not measured per account). Block size 500 vs 2000 gave the same TPS (02 README), consistent with a per-new-account cost in `mine()` and pointing at the engine, not the pipeline.
- Next: revm (approach 05) to test the engine, approach 03 for state-root cost.
